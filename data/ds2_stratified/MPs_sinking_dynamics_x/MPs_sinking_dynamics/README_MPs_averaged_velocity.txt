1. Title of Dataset: MPs_averaged_velocity
Data were collected in Laboratory of Hydrodynamic Micromodels, Institute of Geophysics, Polish Academy of Sciences

3. Methodological information:

Detailed description of methods used for collection/generation of data: 
Presented in a paper linked to this dataset.

General description: settling of microplastic particles in two-layered column of liquid with upper and lower layer varying in density and/or rheological properties. Solutions composed of artificial seawater and xanthan gum. 6 types of particles wre tested.

4. Data-specific information:
 
Each sub-folder contains:
several .csv files (equal to the number of all runs in the experimental set) called set_NoSet_ParticleType_ind.csv, 
where 
NoSet is a number of set
ParticleType is in the following format:
P_ind where P indicates type of particle (S – sphere, D – disk, R - rod) 
ind - indicates set of particles in terms of dimentsions (a, b, c) defined in the related paper and below,


Files contain avereged sinking velocity assessed based on data on averaged position of particle. Data collected in three columns: 
U [m/s], z [m], t[s]
where U - average sinking velocity, z - vertical coordinate, t - time instant.

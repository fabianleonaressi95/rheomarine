## 2. Contractual architecture — decisive clarification
The Guidelines state (section 1, p. 2, lines 50–57):
- ZRS Koper organises the call on behalf of the RISTANC consortium.
- The consortium remains responsible to the European Commission for the financial-support scheme and compliance with Horizon Europe GA 101217089.
- Recipients are third parties, are NOT beneficiaries of the EU Grant Agreement, and have NO contractual relationship with the European Commission.
- The recipient's rights and obligations are governed exclusively by the funding agreement concluded with ZRS Koper.

This means the recipient's direct contract is with ZRS Koper, not with the European Commission. The payment must be analysed under the ZRS-recipient agreement and Slovenian domestic law, read with the Italy–Slovenia tax treaty.

The call does not publish a template for this funding agreement. It is therefore impossible to confirm its exact legal form, governing law, tax clauses, withholding mechanism, or whether it expressly characterises the payment as a grant, contribution, or consideration for project services.

## 3. What the call documents do establish
### Eligibility and territorial connection
- Natural persons are eligible if adult and permanently resident in an EU Member State (Guidelines §2).
- Project activities must take place in the Slovenian territorial sea, including coast, or contribute to challenges there.
- Selected applicant must have settled outstanding tax liabilities and, where relevant, social-security contributions before signing; the contracting authority verifies this (§2).
- One application per person/entity, whether as applicant or partner.
- No other European, national or local public funding may fund the project or any part of the claimed activities; declaration required.

### Lump sum and own work
- Financial support is EUR 7,500–12,000, up to 100% of estimated project costs; final sum is fixed in the funding agreement (Guidelines §§5.1–5.2).
- The amount may be reduced if budget items are excessive or unnecessary.
- A.3 (FAQ) explicitly permits the applicant's own work to be included, with hours/months and unit cost; it must be budgeted as the full gross cost including all taxes and contributions (bruto bruto / bruto 2).
- FAQ says the tax treatment of the payment to a natural person is governed by Slovenian tax legislation.
- Payments: 20% on signing; 30% after interim milestones; 50% after final results. Payments are tied to activities/results, not proof of actual costs via accounting documents.
- The lump-sum character does not itself decide whether the receipt is taxable income, a grant outside VAT, or consideration for a supply.

### Procurement and external laboratory
- External services and expert reports are eligible (Guidelines §6).
- Procurement must respect transparency, equal treatment and competition even where public-procurement procedures do not apply (Guidelines §11).
- A laboratory quote should state scope, methods, deliverables, total price, VAT status/rate, timetable, and data/IP provisions. The applicant should retain evidence of fair supplier selection even though actual costs are not reimbursed invoice-by-invoice.

### Intellectual property
- Unless later written agreement says otherwise, economic copyright and other IP in work created by the applicant under the contract remain with applicant/author.
- ZRS receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual, worldwide licence for non-commercial research, educational, dissemination, communication and archiving purposes.
- Commercial exploitation by ZRS is not authorised without prior written consent, except the limited non-commercial purposes listed.
- Pre-existing IP should be expressly listed and excluded from assignment/licensing beyond any necessary limited project use. The Guidelines also require open access for qualifying research results where Horizon Europe obligations apply.

## 4. Tax and VAT implications for this applicant scenario
Assumed facts supplied by applicant: Italian tax resident; natural-person applicant; no VAT registration/partita IVA; work performed independently from Italy; no employment relationship with ZRS; no fixed base in Slovenia.

### VAT
- The applicant should answer the form's VAT-registration question truthfully: not VAT-registered, if that remains the actual status.
- No VAT invoice should be issued merely because the grant pays instalments.
- Whether the lump-sum support falls outside the scope of VAT depends on whether there is a direct legal link between payment and an identifiable supply to ZRS/third-party customer. Payment by milestones/results and absence of cost reimbursement support a grant-like analysis but do not conclusively establish it. The agreement's obligations and remedies are decisive.
- VAT on the laboratory supplier's invoice is a separate issue. Do not assume the laboratory budget line is net or VAT-exempt; obtain a written quote with VAT shown and establish who contracts the lab.

### Slovenian income tax and treaty
- FAQ's reference to Slovenian tax law means Slovenian domestic withholding/reporting cannot be dismissed solely because the recipient is Italian and has no VAT number.
- The Guidelines require a tax number in the application/contract preparation context (privacy section §14, lines 463–465); the form is reported to ask for the applicant's tax number. A Slovenian tax number is not a VAT number and does not itself create a VAT registration or permanent establishment.
- FURS procedure for non-residents may require tax registration (DR-02) if the individual earns taxable Slovenian-source income. Whether the payment is taxable in Slovenia must first be determined under domestic law and treaty.
- Article 14 of the Italy–Slovenia treaty is the strongest candidate if the payment is genuinely income from independent scientific/professional activity carried out from Italy and no fixed base is regularly available in Slovenia. It is not automatically triggered by calling the applicant an independent researcher; actual contractual rights/obligations and nature of payment govern.
- Article 22 (other income) is a possible alternative only if the receipt is not dealt with by the treaty's preceding income articles. It should not be asserted as an automatic fallback without testing the treaty classification.
- Article 15 applies only if the legal and factual relationship is employment; the Guidelines' budget label “personnel” or FAQ's “cost of work” is not sufficient to establish employment.
- If Slovenian tax is lawfully withheld despite treaty entitlement, treaty relief/refund procedures and Italian foreign-tax-credit rules may become relevant. Avoid relying on a credit as the first solution if the treaty gives exclusive taxing rights to Italy.

### Social security
- No automatic Slovenian contributions can be inferred from the A.3 label. Determine whether this is employment, self-employment, or a non-employment grant receipt under the actual contract and EU coordination rules.
- The Guidelines' requirement to settle social-security liabilities before signature should be read as a compliance condition; it does not by itself create a Slovenian contribution liability.

## 5. Proposed contractual wording for negotiation (not a claim that ZRS has accepted it)
The beneficiary is an individual tax resident in Italy. The project activities will be carried out independently from Italy, without an employment relationship with ZRS Koper and without a fixed base in Slovenia. The parties acknowledge that the beneficiary is not registered for VAT in Italy or Slovenia [only if factually correct]. The payment is a lump-sum financial contribution linked to the completion and acceptance of the project activities and deliverables, and is not intended to constitute consideration for a supply of services to ZRS Koper [include only if this accurately reflects the parties' actual legal arrangement]. The tax treatment shall be determined under applicable Slovenian law and the Convention between Italy and Slovenia, including Article 14 if the receipt is classified as income from independent scientific/professional activities, or Article 22 only if it is genuinely other income not covered by the preceding articles. Any withholding, registration and reporting duties shall be determined in accordance with the Convention and the beneficiary's non-resident status. No employment relationship, Slovenian fixed base, or VAT registration is created solely by the award.

Important: the sentence describing the payment as not consideration must not be inserted if the substantive obligations show a reciprocal service contract. Substance prevails over labels.

## 6. Questions that the unpublished funding agreement must answer before signature
1. What is the exact legal title and nature of the payment: financial support/grant to a third party, or remuneration for services/deliverables?
2. Does ZRS consider A.3 an employment payment, independent professional income, other income, or a grant? Which Slovenian statutory provision and income category does it apply?
3. Will ZRS withhold Slovenian personal income tax? If yes, what rate, base, and procedure for applying treaty relief before payment?
4. What Slovenian tax number is required, who obtains it, and is DR-02 registration required before first instalment?
5. Does ZRS require a certificate of Italian tax residence and a treaty-relief form (Article 14/22)?
6. Does the agreement contain any obligation to register for Slovenian VAT, issue an invoice, or charge VAT? If no, what document supports the instalment payment?
7. What social-security status does ZRS assume for A.3, and on what legal basis? Is any A1 or Italian INPS documentation required?
8. What are the milestone acceptance criteria, partial-performance reductions, repayment/clawback events, termination rights, and dispute forum/law?
9. Does the agreement preserve all pre-existing IP, software, data, methods and repository materials, with only a limited project licence?
10. What evidence is needed for the 20/30/50% payments and how long must records be retained?

## 7. Recommended position
Do not open a VAT position or issue an invoice just because the call provides A.3. Do not assume that “no VAT” means no tax. Keep the budget gross, with A.3 explicitly all-in as required by the FAQ. Before signing, obtain the agreement and have the tax classification confirmed in writing by a qualified Italian/Slovenian cross-border tax adviser, using the actual contract rather than a generic description of the call.

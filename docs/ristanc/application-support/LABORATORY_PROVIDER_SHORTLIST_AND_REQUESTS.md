## 2. Proposed minimum package within the indicative B.1 allocation (€3,000)

Request itemised prices for each module; do not assume the amount is sufficient.

**Module 1 — rheology (priority)**
- Artificial seawater control plus a small number of xanthan/carrageenan proxy dispersions, concentrations agreed with the laboratory.
- Flow curves (viscosity versus shear rate), controlled and reported temperature, replicates and instrument geometry.
- Raw numerical data, method, uncertainty and signed report.
- Oscillatory tests only as a separately priced option.

**Module 2 — settling (only if technically available and affordable)**
- One or two well-defined particle reference classes.
- Artificial seawater control and selected proxy dispersions.
- Replicated terminal settling measurements under controlled temperature.
- Raw time/position records or validated settling-speed estimates, method and uncertainty.

**Module 3 — particle identification/characterisation (optional)**
- Size and shape description, polymer identification and density only where the method and price are explicit.
- Consider using published particle specifications rather than spending the budget duplicating available characterisation.

**Exclude from the minimum package unless separately approved**
- Natural EPS sampling and analysis.
- Field collection at sea.
- Broad environmental microplastic monitoring.
- Large sample matrices, extensive oscillatory rheology or advanced chemical analysis.

All tests must remain contained in the laboratory. No experimental microplastics or polymer dispersions are to be released into drains or the marine environment.

## 6. Procurement and selection rule

- Send the same technical scope to at least two suitable providers where practicable; follow the exact RISTANC procurement rules and any thresholds confirmed by ZRS.
- Compare technical fit, price including VAT, delivery time, data/raw-output availability, sample logistics, publication rights and conflict-of-interest status.
- Do not list any provider as a confirmed subcontractor before written acceptance/quotation.
- If no suitable service can be obtained within the budget and timeline, narrow the experimental work package and activate the Section C fallback. Do not fill the gap with unsupported assumptions.

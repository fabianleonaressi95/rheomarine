# RheoMarine-SLO — funding pathways beyond RISTANC

**Research snapshot:** 25 November 2026  
**Purpose:** distinguish the immediate pilot application from plausible follow-on funding routes.  
**Important:** this is a funding strategy note, not confirmation that any call is currently open or that the applicant is eligible.

## 1. Immediate instrument — RISTANC

The current application is a small, applicant-led pilot:
- Requested amount: up to EUR 12,000.
- Planned duration: 8 months from the effective date of the funding agreement.
- Applicant: individual resident in an EU Member State.
- Territorial focus: Slovenian territorial sea; the pilot polygon will be fixed after HFR/data-readiness checks.
- Submission deadline: 28 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

The pilot should establish data readiness, a reproducible M0/M1 comparison, the availability of independent validation and whether laboratory evidence justifies an M2 rheological correction.

RISTANC must not be described as a grant to OGS or as an OGS-led application. Any OGS involvement is a separate prospective discussion.

## 2. FVG regional route — Maritime Technologies (S4)

### A1.1.2 — collaborative research, development and innovation

The regional A1.1.2 measure is the closest identified FVG route thematically because it targets the S4 specialisation area **Maritime Technologies / Sustainable Waterborne Mobility and its land connections**.

The published measure is structurally demanding. The official regional summary specifies:
- at least three independent enterprises, including at least two SMEs;
- a university or research organisation as beneficiary, or a research contract worth at least 15% of project cost;
- project duration of 24–48 months;
- minimum eligible project cost of EUR 1,000,000;
- project implementation by beneficiaries at regional premises/operating units.

This is **not** a suitable direct continuation for the current individual applicant or the EUR 12,000 pilot. It is a possible architecture for a later, substantially larger consortium if a future call or successor measure is available and the project can be framed as a market-relevant maritime monitoring/modelling innovation with regional enterprises.

Questions for OGS and regional stakeholders:
1. Is there a forthcoming A1.1.2 window or successor measure?
2. Could an OGS research unit participate, and in what legal/administrative capacity?
3. Which FVG SMEs could have a genuine technical or market role?
4. Could the project produce an applicable service, process or tool aligned with Maritime Technologies S4 trajectories?
5. Which entity would coordinate and carry the application?

Official sources:
- Regione FVG — A1.1.2 overview: https://www.regione.fvg.it/rafvg/cms/RAFVG/ricerca/fare-ricerca/FOGLIA11/
- Regional call page and requirements: https://www.regione.fvg.it/rafvg/cms/RAFVG/ricerca/fare-ricerca/FOGLIA11/articolo.html

## 3. FVG Proof of Concept — A1.3.1

A1.3.1 supports proof-of-concept development from previous research and development results, with the objective of increasing technology readiness and moving towards innovative products/services.

The Region currently states that no new application windows are expected because funds are exhausted. Therefore this is **not an immediately available route**. Monitor a possible successor instrument; a future application would first need an evidenced research result and a credible route towards a usable product or service.

Official source:
https://www.regione.fvg.it/rafvg/cms/RAFVG/ricerca/fare-ricerca/FOGLIA13/articolo.html

## 4. FVG TRL 6–8 validation measure — not a current fit

The 2026 regional TRL 6–8 measure is not a natural fit for the present project: its official scope is the S4 area “Health, Quality of Life, Agri-food and Bioeconomy”, with specified trajectories. The 2026 first window closed on 21 September 2026. Do not present it as an available RheoMarine funding route unless a future call changes its scope and eligibility.

Official source:
https://www.regione.fvg.it/rafvg/cms/RAFVG/ricerca/fare-ricerca/FOGLIA21/

## 5. Italy–Slovenia cross-border cooperation

A cross-border route is conceptually relevant because the research question, data infrastructure and potential partners span the northern Adriatic and the Italy–Slovenia cooperation area.

Potential framing:
- marine litter and microplastic monitoring;
- interoperability of oceanographic and environmental datasets;
- decision-support for coastal monitoring;
- joint validation protocols and shared technical methods.

This route requires a call-specific check: eligible territories, lead-partner rules, minimum partnership composition, eligible costs, co-financing rates, thematic priorities and deadlines must be taken from the exact open call. Do not state that a specific call is open or that the current individual applicant can lead a consortium without verifying those conditions.

Official programme portal: https://www.ita-slo.eu/

## 6. European research programmes

For a more mature project, monitor:
- Horizon Europe topics on ocean observation, marine pollution, biodiversity, coastal resilience and the EU Mission “Restore our Ocean and Waters”.
- Relevant Mission Ocean or other European partnership calls, where work programme and consortium rules match.
- Other transnational marine-science calls only where geographic scope and applicant types fit.

These are follow-on opportunities, not a substitute for the immediate RISTANC pilot. A competitive application would require a consortium, a well-defined scientific work plan, access to independent data and a credible experimental/field validation component.

Official starting points:
- EU Funding & Tenders Portal: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/
- EU Mission “Restore our Ocean and Waters”: https://mission-ocean-and-waters.ec.europa.eu/

## 7. Recommended staged strategy

| Stage | Objective | Required evidence / partners | Funding route |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Pilot (2026–2027) | Establish data feasibility, M0/M1 comparison and validation requirements | Applicant-led workflow; public HFR; independent data access; bounded lab quote if feasible | RISTANC |
| 2. Consolidation | Produce stronger experimental and independent validation evidence; define a usable monitoring/modelling service | Research organisation, qualified laboratory, data custodians, potential user organisation | Future FVG or Italy–Slovenia call, subject to call rules |
| 3. Scale-up | Multi-site, multi-year research and demonstration with regional economic relevance | Research consortium and enterprise/research composition required by the specific call | FVG Maritime Technologies A1.1.2 successor or suitable European programme |

## 8. Financial and legal safeguards

- Do not charge the same activity or cost to two public funding sources.
- Respect RISTANC's double-funding declaration and budget rules.
- A future application must define distinct work packages, periods and costs. Prior RISTANC outputs may be used as background results, but the same costs cannot be claimed again.
- Do not describe a laboratory, OGS, NIB, ARSO, university or company as a committed partner without written confirmation.
- Do not promise a future regional application before identifying an eligible lead applicant and a consortium satisfying the actual call.
- Preserve the applicant's pre-existing code, methods, repositories and datasets as background IP; negotiate future consortium IP terms explicitly.

## 9. Position to communicate to OGS

> The immediate RISTANC application is a small, independent pilot intended to test feasibility and produce a defensible evidence base. If the pilot demonstrates value, the next step could be a larger collaborative programme. The FVG Maritime Technologies instrument is a particularly relevant regional model, but its previous version required a million-euro consortium with at least three enterprises and a research organisation. We would therefore like OGS's advice on whether a future consortium of this kind is realistic, which regional companies could have a genuine role, and whether an Italy–Slovenia or European route would be more suitable.

# RISTANC — responsible AI, ethics and NODC data-use note
**Project:** RheoMarine-SLO  
**Review date:** 28 November 2026  
**Purpose:** operational checklist for Section B and the implementation of Section C. This note is not a substitute for the official RISTANC Ethical Aspects document or the applicant's declarations.

## 1. Responsible use of AI

The application may describe bounded AI assistance transparently. Proposed controls:
- AI may support language editing, code explanation, literature discovery and administrative drafting.
- AI output is not evidence and is not accepted without verification against primary sources, original datasets, calculations and reproducible tests.
- The applicant remains solely responsible for scientific interpretation, code, citations, results and conclusions.
- No AI system is credited as an author.
- Do not upload personal data, confidential correspondence, unpublished third-party material, restricted datasets or identifiable stakeholder information to public AI services.
- Record materially relevant AI assistance in the methods/reproducibility log: tool and version where known, purpose, date, human verification and affected deliverable.
- Label synthetic or AI-generated data explicitly; do not mix it with observations or laboratory measurements.

This is a project-level integrity safeguard, not a claim that the call has a dedicated AI-use permission or exemption. The current RISTANC call page identifies Ethical Aspects as a required version-2 document; the official ethical document and Slovenian text prevail.

## 2. Ethical commitments and Section B

The application form requires the applicant to:
1. declare any actual, potential or perceived conflict of interest, including relevant ties involving partners, employees or proposed subcontractors, and promptly notify the coordinator if a conflict arises;
2. acknowledge having read the Ethical Aspects document, undertake to respect its commitments, and be prepared to participate in the RISTANC research process;
3. declare whether the project has received or will receive other European, national or local public funding.

The acknowledgement of Ethical Aspects is an applicant commitment. The conflict-of-interest and double-funding boxes are factual declarations and must be selected only after the applicant has personally checked the facts. Do not pre-tick them in a draft. The applicant must also review the separate informed-consent form at grant-agreement stage and any consent/information notices before collecting stakeholder or participant data.

## 3. NODC portal and third-party data

The OGS National Oceanographic Data Centre (NODC) portal may be used as a discovery/access route, subject to the applicable data policy and each record's access restriction and owner-specific conditions.

NODC's published Data Policy states:
- information, data, products and routines are available under the owner/producer's policy conditions;
- acknowledgement of data providers/owners and the data centre is required when data are used in a publication;
- substantial use of an individual source may warrant considering an offer of co-authorship through personal contact with the provider/owner;
- data may not be redistributed to third parties.

Operational controls:
- check each record's access restriction before use or download;
- record dataset ID, provider/owner, retrieval date, licence/terms, permitted purpose and attribution;
- contact the data owner where access is restricted or where substantial use makes collaboration/co-authorship discussion appropriate;
- do not republish raw NODC data in GitHub or project outputs unless the owner explicitly permits redistribution;
- publish project-generated code, metadata and derived products only to the extent allowed by source terms.

Informal guidance from contacts in Trieste that the portal may be used is recorded as practical guidance, not a blanket licence, data-owner consent or institutional partnership.

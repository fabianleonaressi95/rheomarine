## Scope and supplier confirmation

The supplier confirms in writing that its natural-water µFTIR method can be used for seawater from the Gulf of Trieste. The quoted analytical service includes sample preparation and reporting. The report includes polymer type, particle count and particle size. A field blank is accepted but is charged as a full sample. Sampling and shipping require microplastic-free sample containers. Expected turnaround is 4–5 weeks. Measurlabs confirms it can accept an order from an individual applicant as well as a company.

The requested pilot batch is five samples: four seawater samples (two candidate coastal locations, duplicate samples at each location) and one field blank.

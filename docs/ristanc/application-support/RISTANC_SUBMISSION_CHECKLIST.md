# RISTANC submission checklist — RheoMarine-SLO

**Status snapshot: 26 November 2026.** This checklist is aligned with the revised official call documents (version 2, 28 September 2026) and the official Q&A updated 5 November 2026. The Slovenian official text prevails if translations differ.

## 1. Submission package

- [ ] Transfer the completed content from RISTANC_APPLICATION_FORM_COMPLETED_DRAFT.md and SECTION_C_DRAFT_RHEOMARINE_SLO_EN.md into the official Application Form v2 in English or Slovenian.
- [ ] Complete all applicable applicant details in Section A: use A.1 for a legal entity or A.2 for a natural person; complete A.3/contact details as applicable.
- [ ] Complete Section B: conflict-of-interest declaration, acknowledgement of Ethical Aspects and double-funding declaration.
- [x] Section C content drafted in full, including quantified HFR data-readiness, applicant competencies and Measurlabs offer; transfer into official form and verify final layout/character limits. If partners are included, identify them and their roles in the partner section (C.7).
- [ ] Complete the financial plan and check the arithmetic and eligibility of each cost category.
- [ ] Sign the application as the applicant or authorised legal representative.
- [ ] Attach the annexes applicable to the applicant type, as identified in the form:
  - [ ] AJPES extract or other competent register extract — **legal entities only**.
  - [ ] Articles of association/founding document — where relevant to the legal entity.
  - [ ] Power of attorney — only if the application is submitted by someone other than the legal representative/applicant.
- [ ] Convert the signed form and required attachments to PDF for submission.

The form does not list a general identity-card or residence-certificate attachment. Do not add one as a mandatory annex unless the current official instructions or the organiser specifically requests it. Eligibility still requires a natural-person applicant to be an adult with permanent residence in an EU Member State.

## 2. Scientific and territorial consistency

- [ ] Define the pilot area and activities in relation to the Slovenian territorial sea/coast; do not rely on “North Adriatic” alone.
- [ ] Keep HFR surface-current evidence distinct from vertical circulation and seabed deposition.
- [ ] Distinguish published-data analyses, software verification, data readiness, independent laboratory evidence and predictive validation.
- [ ] State the DS1/DS2 dataset limitations and do not imply absolute spatial calibration where it is unavailable.
- [ ] Do not present hypotheses or model outputs as independent physical validation.
- [ ] Separate calibration data from independent validation data and state metrics/uncertainty.
- [ ] Do not claim OGS, NIB, FVO, IzVRS, a university or a laboratory as a confirmed project partner without written agreement.
- [x] Written Measurlabs M49249 quotation received and checked: five-sample µFTIR scope; EUR 2,047 before customer-country VAT; indicative EUR 2,497.34 including 22% Italian VAT. It remains a supplier offer, not a completed analysis or partner commitment.

## 3. Budget checks

- [ ] Requested contribution is between EUR 7,500 and EUR 12,000.
- [ ] A.3 is expressed as the applicant's total applicable gross cost (“bruto bruto”/gross 2), including applicable taxes and contributions; it is not a net take-home amount.
- [x] B.1 scope and supplier basis documented using Measurlabs M49249; final VAT treatment must still be confirmed before ordering.
- [ ] C.1–C.6 are itemised and linked to activities; no double counting with supplier-provided consumables.
- [ ] Indirect costs are calculated as 25% × (A + C), excluding B.
- [ ] Total costs are calculated as A + B + C + E and reconciled with the requested lump sum.
- [ ] Any other project income/contribution is disclosed in the income section.
- [ ] Keep quotes, selection rationale, contracts/orders and deliverables as evidence even though the call is lump-sum and not reimbursement of actual invoices.
- [ ] Obtain professional advice on the applicant's tax/social-security treatment and verify the funding agreement before signing; the repository's tax note is not a tax ruling.

## 4. Submission and receipt

- [ ] Send the application and required annexes in PDF by email to **ristanc-funding@zrs-kp.si**.
- [ ] Use the subject format: **RISTANC – application for the call [applicant name]**.
- [ ] Send no later than **28 November 2026, 23:59 CET**.
- [ ] Save the exact sent email, attachment set and file hashes/date in the project archive, excluding unnecessary sensitive personal data.
- [ ] Obtain written receipt confirmation. The official call page warns that applications sent during the Microsoft outage on 13 November were not received; absent confirmation, resend and request confirmation.
- [ ] Check that the confirmation identifies the correct applicant and application.

## 5. After submission

- [ ] Prepare for a possible oral presentation (expected around 10 December 2026); do not introduce new project content or increase the requested contribution at that stage.
- [ ] Monitor selection publication by **15 December 2026**.
- [ ] If selected, provide written acceptance within 10 days of notification.
- [ ] Review the funding agreement before signing, including milestones, payment tranches, acceptance/reduction rules, tax treatment, IP and pre-existing materials.
- [ ] Confirm settled tax and social-security obligations where applicable before agreement signature.
- [ ] Set the project start to the agreement signature date, never earlier than **1 January 2027**; duration must be 6–12 months and completion no later than **28 February 2028**.
- [ ] Submit the final project report within **30 days after project completion**.

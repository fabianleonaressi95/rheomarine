## Project-specific evidence

### 1. RheoMarine — published experimental datasets and computational analysis

Repository: https://github.com/fabianleonaressi95/RheoMarine

The project contains scripts and reproducible outputs analysing two published datasets concerning microplastic settling in polymer-modified and stratified fluids:

- **DS1:** analysis of 722 particle-tracking trajectories, deriving terminal settling velocities and retardation ratios relative to the corresponding artificial-seawater baseline.
- **DS2:** analysis of 108 averaged trajectories across stratified-fluid interface zones, comparing settling speeds above and below the interface and retaining the limitations of the set-to-fluid mapping.

Relevant scripts and outputs include:
- `scripts/analyze_ds1_settling.py`
- `scripts/analyze_ds2_stratified.py`
- `analysis/POST_MERGE_AUDIT_2026-11-25.md`
- `data/ds1_rheology_settling/ds1_analysis_summary.json`
- `data/ds2_stratified/ds2_interface_summary.json`

**What this demonstrates:** computational processing of experimental particle trajectories, derivation of settling metrics, comparison across material/fluid conditions, and explicit audit of limitations.

**What it does not demonstrate:** that the applicant performed the underlying experiments, operated rheometers, collected marine samples or independently validated a predictive model in the Slovenian sea.

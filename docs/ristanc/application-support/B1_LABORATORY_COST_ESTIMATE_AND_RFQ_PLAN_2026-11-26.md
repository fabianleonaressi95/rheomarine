# RheoMarine-SLO — B.1 Laboratory Cost Estimate and RFQ Plan
**Date:** 26 November 2026  
**Status:** Preliminary market-based estimate; not a supplier quotation, order, reservation or evidence of provider availability.

## 1. Procurement approach

The B.1 allocation is €3,000. A defensible application budget can be built from public price references, provided the application clearly labels the amount as an estimate and does not imply that a supplier has accepted the work.

Priority should be given to Slovenia-based providers able to confirm seawater-matrix suitability and sample preparation. The public tariff below is a benchmark, not an assumption that the provider will accept an individual applicant or the exact proposed protocol.

| Natural/environmental water µFTIR | 5 samples | €390 | €1,950 |
| Order/service fee | 1 | €97 | €97 |
| Shipping and protective packaging allowance | 1 estimate | €150 | €150 |
| **Indicative subtotal before VAT** | | | **€2,197** |

A prudent provisional ceiling is **€3,000 all-in**. The tax amount must not be stated as settled until the selected provider confirms its invoicing treatment and the applicant's purchasing status. As a sensitivity illustration only, applying 25.5% to the entire €2,197 subtotal gives €2,757.24. This is not a tax determination: cross-border VAT treatment, customer classification, shipping treatment and any reverse-charge rules must be confirmed by the supplier and a tax adviser. Do not insert an assumed 22% Italian VAT rate as if it were necessarily applicable.

The €150 shipping allowance is a planning estimate, not a carrier quotation. Replace it with a carrier quote or the supplier's shipping charge before finalising the budget.

## 4. Proposed sampling design — subject to laboratory approval

A five-unit analytical batch could consist of:
- four environmental seawater samples: two candidate pilot locations, with duplicate samples at each location;
- one field blank to assess contamination introduced during field handling and transport.

This design is provisional. The laboratory must confirm whether it can process the field blank as a full analytical sample, whether 500 mL is adequate for the expected particle load, whether seawater salinity/organic matter requires additional preparation, and whether the same tariff applies to blanks and environmental samples. If the blank is not processed by µFTIR, ask for a separately priced blank-control protocol and revise the sample count/cost accordingly.

The five samples are a small pilot for method feasibility and polymer identification; they do not constitute a statistically representative survey of the Slovenian coastal sea.

## 5. Minimum requested analytical outputs

Ask each provider to specify whether the service includes:
1. sample receipt, storage and chain-of-custody requirements;
2. contamination-control protocol and blank handling;
3. filtration, digestion or other sample preparation, including consumables;
4. visual/optical screening and µFTIR/FTIR polymer identification;
5. size classes and lower detection/identification limit;
6. polymer library and match/acceptance criteria;
7. results per sample, units, raw spectra or machine-readable output where available;
8. QC, uncertainty/limitations and a signed analytical report;
9. turnaround time and delivery date;
10. full price including VAT/taxes, shipping, preparation and any additional fees;
11. permission to report and publish aggregated/anonymised results in the project outputs.

Do not assume FTIR alone measures aggregation kinetics, effective density, settling velocity or rheological parameters. Those require separate methods and a separate technical specification.

## 6. Procurement decision rule and fallback

- **Preferred:** obtain a written, all-inclusive offer from IzVRS or FVO that fits the €3,000 ceiling and the M4 schedule.
- **Benchmark/fallback:** use the Measurlabs price only if the supplier confirms it can contract with the applicant's legal/purchasing status and confirms suitability for Slovenian seawater samples.
- **Alternative benchmark:** CCMAR's published price may inform a later enquiry but is not a secured offer.
- If no provider can deliver a technically appropriate service within the budget and schedule, remove or reduce the new laboratory campaign. Preserve M0 and the evidence-based literature/sensitivity work; do not describe literature-derived values as newly measured project data.

## 7. Suggested budget wording

> B.1 — External laboratory analytical services: up to €3,000. The amount is a preliminary market-based estimate for a small pilot batch of up to five seawater/quality-control samples, including sample preparation, microplastic screening and polymer identification by an appropriate FTIR-based method, subject to provider confirmation. The estimate is informed by publicly available European laboratory tariffs and will be finalised against a written quotation covering method suitability, sample requirements, analytical outputs, turnaround time, shipping and applicable taxes. If the required service cannot be procured within the ceiling and project schedule, the laboratory component will be reduced or omitted and no unperformed measurements will be claimed.

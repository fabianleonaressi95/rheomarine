## Qualifications and open points
- The offer table excludes VAT and other applicable taxes. The supplier email says the customer's national VAT applies and Measurlabs will not add taxes. Confirm the effective VAT treatment for an Italian-resident natural-person applicant and VAT eligibility with ZRS before fixing B.1.
- New customers pay in advance.
- The email calls the €97 fee the only extra service cost, but the general terms make the customer responsible for secure packaging, shipping and insurance in transit. Clarify shipping arrangements and any cost in writing.
- The fee includes project management, reporting, sample handling, storage and disposal. Samples may be destroyed after analysis unless retention/return is agreed in writing.
- The quotation is not an order; no samples have been commissioned or analysed.

## Project use
This is a concrete supplier quotation for WP2/B.1 and can support D2 (independent analytical characterisation) once results are received and quality-checked. It does not establish sampling permissions, sample availability, a final sampling design, physical validation of HFR, or a scientific partnership. The five-sample design is the applicant's proposed pilot, not a plan prescribed by the supplier.

**Budget recommendation:** retain five samples (€2,047 before VAT/logistics) as the documented base case; do not expand the batch until VAT, logistics, sample plan and funding eligibility are resolved.

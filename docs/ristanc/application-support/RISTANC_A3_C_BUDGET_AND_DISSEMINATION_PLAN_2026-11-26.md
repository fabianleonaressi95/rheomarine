## 1. Proposed A.3 calculation

The applicant is applying as a natural person. The RISTANC Q&A confirms that the applicant's own project work is eligible under A.3 and that the form must state the planned workload (hours or months) and unit cost. The amount must be the full cost including all applicable taxes and social contributions (Slovenian terminology: bruto bruto / bruto 2). The actual tax treatment of the payment to a natural person is governed by Slovenian tax law.

| A.3 item | Quantity | Unit | All-in unit cost | Total |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Applicant's direct project work | 220 | hours | EUR 30.00/hour | **EUR 6,600** |

The 220 hours correspond to an average of 27.5 hours per month over eight months. The EUR 30/hour figure is a **budget-planning all-in unit cost**, not a statement of the applicant's net hourly pay. It must be treated as the gross total cost inclusive of applicable taxes and contributions; it must not be increased by adding taxes again.

It assigns no amount to equipment, consumables or miscellaneous purchases because the current plan assumes use of existing computing equipment and places any laboratory consumables within the B.1 laboratory quote unless explicitly excluded.

| C.5 — Costs of communication activities | **EUR 240** | Slovenian translation/proofreading of the two-page public summary (planning allowance EUR 140) and a small print run/distribution of the summary (planning allowance EUR 100). | The applicant prepares the scientific content and layout as part of A.3; C.5 pays only for external language support and physical dissemination materials. |
| C.6 — Other | **EUR 0** | No other direct purchase currently identified. | Do not use as a balancing figure. |
| **Total C** | **EUR 600** |  |  |

### C-cost safeguards

1. These are estimated allocations for the application, not supplier quotes or a promise to incur every item.
2. Keep the four travel trips only if they are genuinely required by the final pilot and work plan. The EUR 60/trip assumption must be checked against the actual starting point, route and travel mode before the form is signed.
3. The briefing should be online or use a no-cost venue if possible. The EUR 120 is a ceiling for necessary room/AV/technical support, not a fee for attendance.
4. The C.5 allowance is for a short public-facing summary and limited circulation, not a commercial advertising campaign. Do not charge the applicant's own writing, layout or dissemination hours again under C.5.
5. The B.1 laboratory supplier quote must state whether consumables, sample preparation, analysis and VAT are included. Remove any overlapping C.3 amount if the supplier includes them.
6. If a cost is unnecessary or cannot be justified, set that sub-line to zero and revise the allocation transparently. Do not invent purchases to reach EUR 600.

## Unit and coordinate contract (verified against `.das` metadata)

- `EWCT`, `NSCT`: **m s⁻¹**, eastward/northward components.
- `latitude`/`longitude`: degrees N/E (EPSG:4326 source).
- `time`: seconds since 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z.
- Projection: local equirectangular, `R = 6371000 m`, centred on the
  subset bbox centre → positions in **metres**. Adequate at ~30 km
  domain scale (< grid spacing error); not a conformal projection.
- Velocity components need no rotation: east/north align with the local
  x/y axes of this projection.

## QC policy

- `valid = (QCflag == 1) & EWCT,NSCT ≠ fill(-32.767) & finite & |v| ≤ 10 m/s`.
  Only flag **1 (good_data)** is accepted; flags 0/2/4/9 and fill -127
  are rejected. In the acquired 7-day files the only non-fill flags
  present are 1 and 4, so this is also the only nonzero choice.
- Invalid cells keep a `0.0` placeholder in the array (required by the
  `VelocityField` finite-value contract) but are masked `valid=False`.
  `VelocityField.sample` raises if a stencil touches them, so the
  placeholder is never used.
- **Missing ≠ zero**: no missing value is ever replaced by an observed
  or synthetic zero velocity.

## Temporal-gap policy

Three distinct gap types are handled, none interpolated across:

1. **Missing timestamps** — `build_segments` splits at gaps >
   `max_gap_s` (default 3 h).
2. **All-invalid timestamps** — timestamps present but with zero
   QC-valid cells (39/337 for IZOL, 19/325 for PIRA in the 7-day
   window) act as barriers: segments break on both sides.
3. **Cell-level QC dropouts** — the pilot driver
   (`simulate_qc_aware`) advances one forcing interval at a time and
   marks a particle `qc_stopped` when its bilinear stencil touches an
   invalid cell at either bracketing time. The trajectory ends at its
   last fully-observed position; the audited implementation checks both
   the starting and advected endpoint stencils before committing a step.

## Pilot design (reproducible)

- Inputs: `data/hfr/raw/EUHFR_NRTcurrent_HFR-NAdr-{IZOL,PIRA}_v3_7d.nc`
  (sha256 recorded in each summary JSON).
- Sub-region: the 4×4-cell window with the highest mean temporal cell
  validity (documented per site in `parameters`).
- Release: deterministic 8×8 candidate grid (64 max), interior 6–94%
  of the sub-region, kept only where the t0 stencil is fully valid.
- Integration: forward Euler, one step per observed forcing interval (~30 min);
  K = 0 (pure advection); reflecting boundaries on the artificial crop window; seed 42.
- Reproduce: `PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/run_hfr_m0_pilot.py --inputs 'data/hfr/raw/*_7d.nc' --out results/hfr_pilot`

## Pilot results (7-day window, Nov 18–25 2026)

| Site | Sub-region mean cell validity | Segments | Longest segment | Result |
|------|------------------------------|----------|-----------------|--------|
| IZOL | 0.884 | 6 | 70.5 h (142 times) | all 64 particles alive, median displacement 3.34 km |
| PIRA | 0.934 | 5 | 70.5 h (136 times) | all 64 alive in longest segment; earlier segments show QC attrition (up to 47/64 stopped) |

Interpretation limits: the baseline displacement values are historical
pre-audit cohort summaries and include QC-stopped particles. They are not
active-only displacement statistics. Regenerate the pilot after merging
the endpoint-QC audit. Reflecting boundaries are an artificial closed-box
assumption on the selected crop, not a coastline condition. Displacements
are advection-only over a few days in a ~6 km sub-region; they demonstrate
pipeline execution, not predictive skill. PIRA's lower coverage produces visible QC-attrition
even inside its best sub-region — consistent with its status as
complementary node pending stronger QC screening.
